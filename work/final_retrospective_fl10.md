## 2. 500-Word Program Retrospective

### **What I Set Out to Do vs. What Changed**
Entering this internship, my primary goal was to strengthen my applied machine learning and system engineering capabilities. Over the course of the track, my understanding shifted from simply building isolated scripts to deploying production-grade, end-to-end architectures. The shift toward strict verification, automated evaluation, and transparent documentation redefined how I approach software development.

### **Key Technical Growth & Engineering Mindset**
The emphasis on "breaking your own site" and rigorous edge-case testing fundamentally changed my development workflow. Rather than assuming a happy-path execution, designing systems with defensive validation, mobile-first responsiveness, and structured evaluation metrics ensured that the tools built were robust and reproducible.

### **Top 3 Transferable Lessons Learned**
1. **Verification Over Assumptions:** Never trust a pipeline without explicit edge-case evaluations and test logs.
2. **Transparent Documentation as Credibility:** High-quality READMEs, clear setup instructions, and honest acknowledgment of limitations build professional credibility.
3. **Iterative Build and Hardening:** Shipping working minimal features first and iteratively hardening them beats over-engineering upfront.
