# Portfolio Sitemap & Toolkit Setup

## 1. Minimal Portfolio Sitemap Strategy

The portfolio focuses strictly on driving a single action: convincing potential collaborators/reviewers to contact for AI/ML engineering roles.

* **Home / Hero Section:** States the core claim — AI Engineering student building end-to-end Machine Learning pipelines with empirical validation.
* **Featured Projects / Proof:** Links to executable notebooks, GitHub repos, and deployed capstone research papers (e.g., FlyRank ML Capstone).
* **About & Toolkit:** Brief summary of technical stack (Python, C, PyTorch/Scikit-Learn, LaTeX) and academic background.
* **Contact / Action:** Direct primary CTA (Email, LinkedIn, GitHub).

---

## 2. Claude Project & Pressure-Test Prompt Audit

### **Custom Instructions (Claude Project):**
> "Act as a technical portfolio tutor and senior ML engineer reviewer. Evaluate all site structures against a single goal: converting visitors to reach out for ML engineering projects. Be concise, critical, and direct."

### **Pressure-Test Prompt:**
> "Pressure-test this 4-part portfolio sitemap against my goal of getting hired/contacted for ML engineering roles: 1) Hero with claim, 2) Executable Capstone Proof, 3) About/Stack, 4) Contact CTA. Does any section waste time? What one thing should I change?"

### **LLM Pressure-Test Output & Refinement:**
* **Feedback:** The structure is clean, but the separation between 'About' and 'Contact' creates unnecessary friction. Combining background stack details directly into the hero/footer eliminates an extra click.
* **Planned Revision:** Merge the "About/Stack" details directly into the Hero and Footer sections to make the landing page a streamlined 1-page narrative.
